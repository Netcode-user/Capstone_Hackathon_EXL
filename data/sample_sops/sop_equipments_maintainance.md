# SOP: Warehouse Equipment Preventive Maintenance

## Step 1: Weekly Inspection
Every forklift and conveyor unit must undergo a documented visual inspection each
Monday morning by the assigned floor technician, logged in the maintenance system.

## Step 2: Fault Reporting
Any fault discovered during inspection must be logged immediately with a severity tag
and the unit tagged "out of service" until repaired, even for minor faults.

## Step 3: Scheduled Servicing
Each unit receives full preventive servicing every 90 days by a certified technician,
following the manufacturer's checklist, regardless of how lightly the unit was used.

## Step 4: Parts Replacement
Only OEM-approved parts may be used for repairs. Substituting third-party parts
requires written sign-off from the maintenance supervisor and must be documented in
the unit's service record with a reason code.

## Step 5: Return to Service
A unit may only be returned to service after the technician signs off the repair in
the maintenance system and a supervisor performs a final safety check.
